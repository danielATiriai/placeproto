PlaceProto: Lexicons for community and place-based apps

PlaceProto is a collective of builders and community organizations working together to define the shared vocabulary of places. PlaceProto is connecting data to places, on three principles.

  1. SimpleWhere we can, we compose PlaceProto using existing lexicons and concepts.
  2. GroundedWe source PlaceProto lexicons from real apps and real users so our vocabulary accurately describes how communities work.
  3. AffordantJust as important as building an open standard is an ecosystem where every neighborhood can define their own lexicon.

We’re looking to build together with companies, developers, community organizations, and neighborhood organizations.
